# Style Sources and Project Adaptation

Reviewed on 11 November 2026. These notes describe the sources and our interpretation; they are not a copied writing manual.

## ASD-STE100

- [Official overview](https://www.asd-ste100.org/about.html).
- [Official FAQ](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html).
- [Official Issue 9 PDF location](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf).

The official material describes writing rules plus a controlled dictionary, with consistent word meanings and provision for technical terms. Its public FAQ identifies concise sentences, a single topic and active voice as principles that can support other writing contexts.

For this project, use those selected principles while preserving software identifiers and necessary technical vocabulary. The full standard and dictionary have not been audited or incorporated into this skill. The PDF and a direct FAQ fetch returned HTTP 403 during authoring; official indexed excerpts and the public overview supplied the limited source basis. Do not claim an exact rule-number audit, approved dictionary coverage, certification or ASD endorsement. A formal compliance request needs the actual applicable standard and a separate review.

## Channel NewsAsia / CNA

- [CNA Explains collection](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/cna-explains).
- [Inspected technology explainer](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/frontier-ai-anthropic-mythos-cybersecurity-risks-cna-explains-6103406), published 7 May 2026.

The inspected article establishes the subject and its significance before explaining terms and implications. It uses short paragraphs, a familiar example and named attribution. These are observations of published writing, not a verified statement of CNA editorial policy. No public CNA house-style manual was found in this research.

Our adaptation uses an answer-first lead, readable context, definitions, evidence and measured conclusions. Do not copy article text, invent expert quotations or add bylines, datelines, news drama or a mandatory question-and-answer format. The article is a style reference; its news claims are not evidence about BugWatch.

## Review the Adaptation

Evaluate factual fidelity first: actor, action, condition, state, limits and uncertainty must survive a rewrite. Then check term explanations, a useful concrete example when needed, proportional detail and source links. Preserve the requested output format. Sentence length alone cannot establish clarity or human understanding.

Forward testing with synthetic facts can reveal an explanation error. It cannot demonstrate better learner comprehension, lower token use, or compliance with either source. Use repeated matched evaluations and actual reader feedback before making those claims.
